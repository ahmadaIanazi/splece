Splece for iPhone and iPad · Effective November 7, 2026

iOS privacy policy

Splece is published by Ahmad Alanazi. You do not need a Splece account. You can plan, edit, preview and export on your device without connecting an AI provider.

Your projects and media

The app keeps project notes, prompts, edits, history, imported files, downloaded results and previews in its local storage. Selecting a photo, video or file imports a copy for the project. Your projects are not automatically uploaded to a Splece cloud account. When you save or share an export, the destination you choose receives that file. Device backups and storage services you enable are separate from Splece.

Provider keys and online requests

If you connect an AI provider, its key is stored in the device Keychain, outside project files. The app uses it to authenticate requests to that provider. Verifying a connection requests account or model information. When you request online writing, analysis or generation, the selected provider and its processing services receive the prompts, instructions, settings and selected reference media needed for that task.

Providers may log requests, retain submitted content and charge your account under their own policies. Review your provider’s privacy and billing terms before submitting sensitive material. Splece saves returned media and the information needed to track jobs locally. Online AI generation does not run entirely on your device.

Your controls and retention

Switch Online off to stop new AI requests. A job already accepted by a provider can still finish and incur charges. Remove the connection in the app to delete its saved key; revoke the key in the provider dashboard to invalidate it there. Contact the provider about deletion of data it already received.

Your local work persists between sessions. To remove all app-local projects and imported copies, first export anything you want to keep, disconnect your keys, then delete the app rather than offloading it. Manage exported files, originals, backups and copies sent to other apps separately. Do not rely on uninstalling alone to revoke a provider key.

Beta feedback and support

If you use TestFlight, Apple handles beta distribution, feedback and diagnostic information under Apple’s privacy policy. Feedback you choose to send can include your message, screenshots and diagnostic information. Remove private project details first.

For a privacy question or request, use Splece support. Public GitHub issues are visible to others: request a private contact without posting personal data. This policy covers the native iOS app. Desktop storage works differently; see the desktop privacy guide.
